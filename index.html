⚠️ A pattern worth sitting with

In your recent entries, warning signs are showing up more often than healthy ones. That doesn't mean the relationship is bad — but it might mean something needs to change, or you need support.

Things that are never okay, even in a bad mood:

  • Being afraid of your partner's reactions
  • Being monitored, tracked, or isolated from friends
  • Being told your reality isn't real (gaslighting)
  • Threats, name-calling, or any physical intimidation
  • Feeling you have to earn basic kindness

If any of this feels familiar, please reach out — a friend, a therapist, or a hotline. You deserve to feel safe. 💗
